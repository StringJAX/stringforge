**Multi-axion EFT: spectra, decay constants, and couplings from Calabi–Yau
compactifications.**

JAXiverse provides a framework-agnostic solver for multi-axion effective field
theories arising from string compactifications. Given the inverse kinetic matrix,
charge matrix, and instanton scales of an N-axion system, it computes masses,
decay constants, mixing matrices, and quartic self-couplings using a choice of
numerical strategies ranging from fast hierarchical approximations to
arbitrary-precision backends.

The package also includes tools for connecting Calabi–Yau geometry to axion
phenomenology, including cosmological observables such as dark-matter relic
densities and axion–photon couplings.
