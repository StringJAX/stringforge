**Numerical Kähler moduli stabilisation for 4d N=1 effective field theories.**

KahlerJAX extends StringJAX to the Kähler-moduli sector of string
compactifications. It provides tools for constructing and stabilising the
Kähler potential of arbitrary four-dimensional N=1 effective field theories
obtained from Calabi–Yau compactifications, including non-perturbative effects
and α′-corrections.

The physics and first applications are described in
[arXiv:2507.00615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00615).
